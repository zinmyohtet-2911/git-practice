Our Story

Founded in 2010, Z Empire has emerged as Myanmar's leading film production company. We began with a simple mission: to elevate Myanmar cinema to international standards while preserving our rich cultural storytelling traditions.Over the past decade, we've produced over 30 feature films, 15 television series, and numerous commercials and documentaries. Our team comprises award-winning directors, cinematographers, and production specialists who are passionate about bringing unique Myanmar stories to life.We believe in the power of film to bridge cultures, inspire change, and showcase the beauty and complexity of Myanmar to the world.
